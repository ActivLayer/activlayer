# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Protect privacy and never publish another person's private information.
- Accept moderation decisions and help keep discussions on topic.

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, sustained disruption, and
other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [community@activlayer.com](mailto:community@activlayer.com).
Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or ban
participants when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
